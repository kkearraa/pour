About pour.

Cocktail recipes that are easy to follow at home.

What pour. is

pour. is a cocktail recipe website for people who want to make good drinks at home without a professional bar. Every recipe shows the glass, method, garnish and ice, and you can scale it to any number of servings and switch between ml, cl and oz.

What you can do here

  • Browse the most popular cocktails, from the Margarita to the Espresso Martini.
  • Tick the bottles you have in the cabinet search and see what you can mix right now, plus drinks you are one ingredient away from.
  • Find zero-proof and low-alcohol drinks with full flavor.
  • Keep it simple with 3-ingredient cocktails.
  • Let the flavor quiz pick a drink for you.
  • Save favorites with the heart button. They stay in your own browser.

Where the recipes come from

The recipes follow the classic versions of each drink. Where the International Bartenders Association (IBA) publishes an official recipe, each recipe popup links to it, so you can compare. Our measurements are rounded for home use and can differ slightly from the official ones.

Who made it

pour. was designed and built by Kira Pugachova as a project for the Interactive Web Technologies course. Questions or ideas? Get in touch.

Drink responsibly

pour. is meant for adults of legal drinking age. If you want to drink less, the zero-proof recipes are a good place to start.
