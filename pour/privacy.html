Privacy Policy

Short version: we don't track you, and almost nothing about you is stored.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who runs this website

pour. is run by Kira Pugachova, who is responsible for this website and the data described here (the "data controller" under the GDPR). You can reach her through the contact page.

The short version

  • No accounts or sign-ups. The only form is "Share your recipe", and you decide what goes in it.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • No newsletter. We will never add you to a mailing list.
  • Your settings and favorites are stored only in your own browser.

What is stored on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember a few things between visits. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • pour_favorites: the drinks you marked with the heart button.
  • theme: whether you chose the light or dark look.
  • pour_cookies_accepted: that you closed the storage notice, so it isn't shown again.

You can delete it at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser settings (often under "Cookies and site data"). The site keeps working; it just forgets your favorites and theme.

If you share a recipe

When you use the "Share your recipe" form on the Community page, we save what you entered in our recipe database: the recipe itself, the name you typed (optional, it can be a nickname) and the photo, if you added one, together with the time it was sent. This is shown publicly on the site so other visitors can make your drink. Please don't put private information in a recipe.

To stop spam, the server briefly remembers the IP address a recipe came from (in memory, for at most one hour, to allow a few recipes per hour). It is not saved in the database.

We keep shared recipes for as long as the site runs. To have yours changed or removed, contact us and tell us the recipe's name.

Our hosting provider

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes, as explained in GitHub's documentation on GitHub Pages. We do not have access to these logs and don't use them.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you. We don't share it, sell it or use it for marketing, and we delete it when the conversation is finished and it is no longer needed.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the International Bartenders Association, Drinkaware and the U.S. National Institutes of Health. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use your data;
  • complain to the data protection authority in your country.

In practice, the only personal data we could hold is an email you sent us or a name you entered with a recipe. To use any of these rights, just contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data (for example, if we ever add a contact form or ads), we will update this page and the date at the top first.
