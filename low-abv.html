person(s)

Drinking less? Swapping a regular drink for a low-alcohol one cuts how much alcohol you have, but only when it replaces a drink rather than adding another one. Low-ABV drinks still contain some alcohol, so choose the zero-proof ones if you want to avoid it completely. For more, see Drinkaware's guide to low and no-alcohol drinks and Rethinking Drinking from the U.S. National Institutes of Health.
